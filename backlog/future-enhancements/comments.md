# Enhancement: Comment System

**Priority:** P3 (Future)  
**Story Points:** 13

## User Story
**As a** blog reader  
**I want** to leave comments on posts  
**So that** I can engage with content

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Comment model (content, author_name, author_email, post_id)
- [ ] Comment form on post pages
- [ ] Comments displayed below post
- [ ] Comment moderation (approve/reject)
- [ ] Spam protection (Akismet or similar)
- [ ] Email notifications for new comments

## Important Note
**Consider external service (Disqus) to avoid complexity**, or skip entirely per original requirements (no comments to reduce edge cases).
